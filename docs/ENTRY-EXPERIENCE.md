# Familiar Entry Experience Purpose

## Summary
Familiar should have a single coherent **entry experience** instead of treating the home screen and chat onramp as separate ideas.

### Core Purpose Statement
**Familiar's entry experience exists to help you quickly resume, choose, or discover what to listen to next, using your recent context first and guided chat/discovery prompts second.**

That means the first screen should answer one core question:

**"What should I do next with my music?"**

Not:
- "How do I browse my library?"
- "Which admin/tool panel should I open?"
- "How do I learn the whole app?"

## What The Entry Experience Should Do
The entry experience should do four things well:

1. **Resume momentum**
- Bring you back to what you were recently doing, playing, or exploring.

2. **Help you start listening quickly**
- Surface a few strong next actions that are immediately playable or openable.

3. **Create an onramp into chat**
- Show that chat is not a blank AI box, but a practical tool for both library guidance and discovery.

4. **Support discovery without overwhelming**
- Offer lightweight ways to branch outward into new artists, releases, or adjacent sounds without turning the home screen into a feed.

## Role Of The Home Screen
The home screen is the **container** for the entry experience.

Its job is:
- to be the default landing surface
- to foreground recent context and likely next actions
- to provide a few high-value modules and shortcuts
- to introduce chat in a way that feels useful immediately

The home screen is **not**:
- a generic library browser
- a full dashboard
- a customizable widget canvas
- a duplicate of Artists/Albums/Tracks views

## Role Of Chat In The Entry Experience
Chat should be presented as a **first-class action surface** within the home screen, not as a side utility.

### Chat Purpose Statement
**Familiar chat helps you decide what to play, revisit, or discover next by combining what it knows about your library with what it can infer about your taste.**

Chat therefore has two equal roles:
- **Library guide**
  - rediscover, resume, refine, and organize listening within your collection
- **Discovery guide**
  - identify missing artists, adjacent sounds, and new releases worth exploring

The prompt onramp is important because it teaches those two roles immediately.

## What Prompt Starters Should Accomplish
Prompt starters on the home screen should:
- reduce blank-page anxiety
- teach the kinds of requests Familiar is good at
- balance library help and discovery help
- feel like normal human requests, not demo prompts

### Starter prompt categories
- Start listening now
- Rediscover my library
- Translate a mood or situation into music
- Find new artists/releases I should explore
- Build a progression or path through music

## Success Criteria
A successful Familiar entry experience should make these statements true:

- "When I open Familiar, I usually see something I actually want to continue or play."
- "I can quickly get back to my recent context without digging through Artists or Albums."
- "The app gives me a few strong next actions instead of making me browse from scratch."
- "Chat feels approachable and obviously useful."
- "I understand that Familiar can help me both use my library better and discover what to explore next."

## Assumptions and Defaults
- The home screen becomes the default landing route.
- Recent personal context is the primary signal.
- Chat/discovery prompts are a core part of the entry experience, not a separate initiative.
- Discovery is a first-class use case, equal to library guidance.
- Customization should remain light and curated, not panel-system-level.
